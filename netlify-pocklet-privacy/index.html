Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

Pocklet is built around a simple principle: your saved things stay yours.

Everything you save lives on your device. We don't sell your data, train AI models on it, or use it for advertising.

What we collect

On your device (local only)

Your saved links, screenshots, categories, notes, locations, reminders, and preferences are stored locally using your device's secure storage.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share a saved item.

Account information (if you sign up)

If you create an account, we store your email address and authentication credentials through Supabase.

Currently, your saved content is not synced to our servers. Your account unlocks unlimited saves beyond the guest experience.

Links you save

When you save a link, we fetch preview images and titles from the source website using third-party services including Microlink, noembed, vxtwitter, TikTok oEmbed, and our own Supabase function.

These requests may involve sending the saved URL to these third-party services and source websites.

Location data

When you add a location to a saved item or search for places, geocoding services Photon and Nominatim resolve addresses and coordinates.

Location data is stored locally and never sent to Pocklet's servers.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and managed entirely on your device. We don't collect or track notification data.

What we don't collect

  • •We don't track your browsing activity
  • •We don't sell your data to third parties
  • •We don't use your data to train AI models
  • •We don't show ads
  • •We don't access your photos unless you explicitly choose to import them

Third-party services

Authentication (Supabase, Google, Apple)

We use Supabase for secure authentication. When you sign up with third-party providers Google or Apple, we only receive the basic profile information needed to create your account, such as your name and email address.

We do not share your saved items or app usage data with these providers. Read Supabase's privacy policy.

Link previews (Microlink, noembed, vxtwitter, TikTok)

When you save a link, we fetch preview images and titles using Microlink, noembed, vxtwitter, TikTok oEmbed, and direct website fetching.

These requests are made from our secure Supabase servers. The URLs you save may be sent to these third-party services and source websites to extract metadata.

Place search (Photon, Nominatim)

When you search for a location, we use open-source geocoding services.

These requests are made directly from your device and may be logged by the service providers.

Maps (OpenStreetMap, Carto)

Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap and Carto. Your device makes requests directly to these services when viewing the map.

We don't collect or store your map usage.

Future features

We're considering adding cloud sync in the future, which would allow you to access your saved items across devices.

If we add this feature:

  • •It will be entirely optional
  • •You'll be notified and asked to opt in
  • •Your data will remain encrypted and private
  • •We'll update this policy with clear details

Your control

Since your data is stored locally, you have complete control:

  • •Delete any saved item at any time
  • •Clear all your data from the app settings
  • •Delete your account and all associated data
  • •Export your data (coming soon)

If you delete the app, all locally stored data is permanently removed from your device.

Account deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

To delete your account, open the Pocklet app, go to Settings > Account, and tap "Delete Account". This action is irreversible and will immediately remove your authentication credentials from our servers.

Please note that since your saved items are stored locally on your device, deleting your account will not automatically delete the data stored on your device. To remove all local data, you must delete the app from your device.

Children's privacy

Pocklet is available to users of all ages. If you're under 13, or under the age of consent in your country, you should use Pocklet with parental guidance.

Because saved content stays on your device and we don't collect personal information beyond your email address if you create an account, Pocklet is designed to keep data collection minimal.

Parents: if your child has created an account and you'd like it removed, contact us at support@getpocklet.com.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as Pocklet evolves.

If we make significant changes, we'll notify you through the app or by email if you've created an account.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when we last revised this policy.

Questions?
